Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Creative Music Television ("CTV", "we", "us") operates CTV Poster, an internal publishing tool used by our own staff to upload CTV's own video content to CTV's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the tool touches and what we do with it.

CTV Poster is not a consumer product. It is not offered to the public, it has no user accounts, and the only social media accounts it connects to are those owned and operated by Creative Music Television.

What we collect

When a CTV staff member connects a CTV social media account to the tool, we receive and store:

  • An access token and refresh token for that account, issued by the platform's authorization server.
  • The account's public identifier (such as the account ID and username) so the tool can confirm it is uploading to the correct account.

We do not collect, request, or store data belonging to any other person. We do not read your followers, your messages, your viewing history, or the content of anyone else's account. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

TikTok data specifically

Our TikTok integration requests a single permission, video.upload. That permission allows the tool to place a video file into the connected account's own TikTok drafts. It does not allow the tool to publish anything — a person opens TikTok and publishes the draft themselves.

We do not download, store, or process TikTok user data beyond the access token and account identifier described above. We do not use TikTok data to train machine learning models, and we do not sell or share it.

How we use it

Access tokens are used for exactly one purpose: to authenticate uploads of CTV's own video files to CTV's own accounts. That is the entire function of the tool.

How we store it

Tokens are stored on CTV-controlled infrastructure with restricted access, transmitted only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and never written into logs, analytics systems, or third-party services. Video files uploaded through the tool are held only long enough to complete the transfer to the destination platform and are then deleted.

How long we keep it

Tokens are retained only while the corresponding account remains connected. Revoking the tool's access from the platform's own settings, or disconnecting the account in our tool, causes the stored token to be deleted. Tokens that expire are discarded.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone. We do not transfer it to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party partners. Data is disclosed only if we are legally required to do so.

Your choices

Because the only accounts connected are CTV's own, the relevant control is CTV's: access can be revoked at any time from the platform's app settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which immediately ends the tool's ability to act on that account.

Children

CTV Poster is an internal business tool and is not directed to or usable by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
