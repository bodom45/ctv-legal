# Oportun leads Texas sub-580 loan options

**Oportun is the best first application** for a Texas borrower with a score under 580 and roughly $5,300-$6,000 a month from Fokus. It is the only lender found that combines no minimum credit score, Texas loans from $300 to $7,500, income-and-expense underwriting, no published job-tenure rule, and an APR ceiling near 36% ([Oportun Texas page](https://information.oportun.com/txloans/); [LendingTree](https://www.lendingtree.com/personal/reviews/oportun/)). Apply to the nonprofit Capital Good Fund alongside it, because it is cheaper (12%-18% APR, no fees, no minimum FICO) but has a lower cap and unknown funding speed ([Capital Good Fund](https://capitalgoodfund.org/impact-loan-overview/)). If both decline, a Texas storefront installment lender such as Sun Loan, Western Shamrock, World Finance or Security Finance is the near-certain fallback at roughly 85%-110% APR; OppLoans and Rise are the online equivalents at 99%-199%. Income is not the obstacle at any of these lenders. The score, the short history, and whether pay is W-2 or 1099 decide the outcome. A business loan is not a realistic route: the business lenders found require a 600 score or six months of revenue. No source gives approval rates by score band, so this ranking rests on published underwriting criteria. Every figure comes from search-result summaries rather than full page reads, so confirm each one with the lender before signing.

## Income clears every floor; score and history decide

Earnings of $16,000-$18,000 over three months exceed every stated or rumoured income minimum the researchers found. Those range from about $1,200 a month at Avant to $1,500 a month at OppLoans and $12,000 a year at Oportun and Upstart ([MoneyLion](https://www.moneylion.com/learn/avant-personal-loans); [SuperMoney](https://www.supermoney.com/oportun-vs-upstart)). What remains is the score, existing debts, and the condition of the bank account, since cash-flow lenders look at deposits and overdrafts.

Three months of history is tight but workable:

- **No tenure rule found:** no lender publishes a job-tenure requirement longer than three months.
- **OppLoans:** third-party reviews cite a bank account about 90 days old plus direct deposit ([OppLoans FAQ](https://opploans.com/faqs/what-are-the-requirements-and-eligibility-to-apply-for-a-loan); [Finder](https://www.finder.com/short-term-loans/opploans-loans)).
- **Capital Good Fund:** two months of bank history through Plaid.
- **Upstart:** a pay stub from the last 30 days, or an offer letter, for W-2 applicants ([Upstart help](https://upstarthelp.upstart.com/borrower-verification/documents-needed-to-apply-for-a-loan)).

The W-2 versus 1099 question matters more than the three months. Upstart and OneMain verify self-employment income with tax returns, which will not yet show Fokus income ([Upstart guide](https://www.upstart.com/learn/income-based-loans/); [LendingTree](https://www.lendingtree.com/personal/reviews/onemain-financial)). A 1099 borrower should therefore lean on Oportun, Capital Good Fund and the storefront lenders. Sun Loan accepts bank statements from self-employed applicants ([Sun Loan Texas](https://www.sunloan.com/online-loans/texas/)).

## The ranked order: cheapest plausible approval first

The order below runs from cheapest to dearest, so a decline costs only time. Oportun's prequalification is a soft pull, and Sun Loan and World Finance also prequalify with a soft inquiry. A soft-pull rate check at the others was not confirmed in this research.

| Order | Lender | Texas amount | Reported cost | Fit for this borrower |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oportun** | $300-$7,500, 7-49 months | APR up to about 35.99%; administrative fee up to 10% | No score or credit file needed; best blend of odds and cost |
| 1 (parallel) | **Capital Good Fund** | $300-$1,500 (United Way Dallas says up to $2,500) | 12%-18% APR, no fees, 15 months | No minimum FICO; cheapest; speed and county coverage unknown |
| 2 | **Community Loan Center** | Up to $1,000, 12 months | 18% plus $20 fee | Only if Fokus is an enrolled employer |
| 3 | **OneMain Financial** | $1,500-$30,000 | About 12%-35.99% APR plus origination fee | No score floor; collateral or a co-applicant helps; W-2 preferred |
| 4 | **Upstart** | $1,000-$75,000 | 6.10%-35.99% APR; origination fee 0%-12% | No formal floor, but approvals skew 580+ |
| 5 | **Storefront installment lenders** | About $450-$1,840 | About 80%-113% APR | Built for this credit tier; same-day decisions |
| 6 | **OppLoans / Rise** | $500-$4,000 or $5,000 | 99%-199% APR | Highest online approval odds; most expensive acceptable option |

### 1. Oportun and Capital Good Fund

**Oportun** requires government ID, an SSN or ITIN, proof of residency and proof of income. It weighs earnings against housing and other expenses, and it reports payments to credit bureaus ([LendingTree](https://www.lendingtree.com/personal/reviews/oportun/); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/personal-loans/reviews/oportun-personal-loans)). Reported APR ranges conflict (19.9%-36% in some sources, 31.95%-35.95% in others), so a sub-580 borrower should expect the top of the range ([SuperMoney](https://www.supermoney.com/reviews/personal-loans/oportun-personal-loans)). No verified time-to-fund figure was found.

**Capital Good Fund** lists Texas as a served state. It asks for a bank account, income, two months of bank history and ID ([Capital Good Fund](https://capitalgoodfund.org/impact-loan-overview/)). Its own pages conflict on the Texas price: one says 12%, another 18%, and a third says APR varies by county ([Texas page](https://capitalgoodfund.org/overview/impact-loans-tx-2/)). The maximum also conflicts: $1,500 on its pages versus "up to $2,500" from its partner United Way of Metropolitan Dallas ([United Way Dallas](https://unitedwaydallas.org/resources/affordable-loans/)). A $1,000 request fits under either cap.

### 2. Community Loan Center, if Fokus participates

One question to Fokus HR settles this. The program lends up to $1,000 at 18% plus a $20 fee, repaid by payroll deduction, with no credit history needed and three months on the job required ([CLC of the Brazos Valley](https://clcbv.org/); [BCL of Texas](https://bcloftexas.org/community/loan-centers)). The loan is available only to employees of enrolled employers, and nothing in the research indicates whether Fokus is one. The City of Houston version requires full-time employment, which suggests W-2 status ([City of Houston FAQ](https://www.houstontx.gov/hr/hrfiles/benefits/FAQ-%20CLC.pdf)). Most term sources are several years old.

### 3-4. OneMain and Upstart

**OneMain** publishes no minimum score and has Texas branches. Its $1,500 minimum means borrowing more than needed, and its origination fee varies by state ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/reviews/loans/personal-loans/onemain-financial-personal-loans)).

**Upstart** has no formal floor, but one review notes most approvals are 580 or higher, so odds here are low to moderate ([LendingTree](https://www.lendingtree.com/personal/installment-loans-for-bad-credit/); [Finder](https://www.finder.com/personal-loans/best-bad-credit-personal-loans)).

Avant, Upgrade and Universal Credit report floors of roughly 550-580 and are a coin flip at best ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/loans/avant-review/)). NetCredit (34%-99.99% APR, $1,000 minimum, no score floor) is a reasonable middle step, but its Texas availability was not confirmed from its own pages ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/loans/personal-loans/reviews/netcredit)).

### 5. Storefront installment lenders

These lenders publish no minimum score, market to bad credit, and ask only for ID, a pay stub and proof of residence.

- **Sun Loan:** up to $1,700 in Texas at 83.01%-119.12% APR over 4-18 months, usually funded the same day ([Sun Loan](https://www.sunloan.com/personal-installment-loans/)).
- **Western Shamrock:** up to $1,840, with approval within 30 minutes of receiving documents ([Western Shamrock](https://westernshamrock.com/locations/texas)).
- **Security Finance:** $448-$1,801 ([Security Finance](https://www.securityfinance.com/about-our-loans/)).
- **World Finance:** reports to all three credit bureaus ([World Finance](https://www.loansbyworld.com/loans/loans-for-building-credit)).

The Texas regulator puts this loan class at about 80%-113% APR ([OCCC 2025 study](https://occc.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025_study_consumer_loan_products.pdf)). The researchers estimate about $580 in charges on $1,000 over 12 months. That estimate assumes an acquisition-charge formula they could not confirm.

### 6. OppLoans and Rise

**OppLoans** quotes 99%-195% APR on its Texas page, while NerdWallet and LendingTree report 160%-195%. It has no minimum score and no hard pull ([OppLoans Texas](https://www.opploans.com/personal-loans-in-texas); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/personal-loans/reviews/opploans-personal-loans)).

**Rise** lists $500-$5,000 at 99%-199% on its Texas pages ([Rise Dallas](https://www.risecredit.com/loans/dallas)).

By the researchers' own amortization, $1,500 at 160% over 12 months costs about $1,590 in interest, versus about $625 at 35.99% over 24 months. Early payoff cuts the cost; OppLoans reports no prepayment fee.

## Business loans are a dead end at three months

The business route fails on the same score and adds a time-in-business hurdle.

- **Fundbox:** lowest published minimums are a 600 personal score, three months in business and $30,000 annual revenue. Other reviews say six months and $100,000 ([Business.org](https://www.business.org/finance/loans/fundbox-review/); [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/loans/small-business/reviews/fundbox/)).
- **Lendio:** general floor of a 520 score, $8,000 monthly revenue and six months in operation ([Finder](https://www.finder.com/business-loans-like-fundbox)).
- **Giggle Finance:** the only plausible 1099 option. It underwrites on connected bank data and advertises up to $15,000. Sources conflict on whether it has no credit minimum or requires 600+ and six months in business. One review reports a 7% origination fee, and its factor rates are unpublished, so the true cost cannot be stated ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/loans/reviews/giggle-finance); [Finder](https://finder.com/business-loans/giggle-finance-review); [United Capital Source](https://www.unitedcapitalsource.com/business-loans/lender-reviews/giggle-finance-review/)).

A W-2 employee has no business to underwrite. For a 1099 contractor, a personal loan from the ranked list is both likelier and cheaper.

## What to avoid

**Payday and auto title loans from Credit Access Businesses.** Texas does not cap their fees. The regulator's 2024 study puts installment payday loans at about 488% APR and title loans at about 272% ([OCCC 2024 study](https://occc.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2024_study_consumer_loan_products_0.pdf)). Refinances made up 44% of 2024 loan volume ([Texas Fair Lending Alliance](https://www.texasfairlending.org/the-basics)). News reports citing Texas Appleseed say more than 25% of title borrowers lost a car in 2024; the regulator's own figure, on a different base, is about 4.2% of active title loans ([NBC DFW](https://www.nbcdfw.com/investigations/squeezed-struggling-families-payday-lenders-often-unwilling-to-negotiate-missed-payments/3943295/?amp=1)). On the regulator's disclosure-form averages, $1,000 costs roughly $1,000-$1,400 in fees over four to five months (researcher arithmetic).

**Lenders that cannot deliver $1,000 or cost more than the fallbacks.**

- **Possible Finance:** capped at $750 in Texas ([Possible Texas](https://www.possiblefinance.com/states/texas)).
- **Integra Credit:** up to 399% APR ([LendingTree](https://lendingtree.com/personal/reviews/integra-credit)).
- **CreditNinja:** reported APRs range from 35.99% to 349%, too inconsistent to rely on ([SuperMoney](https://www.supermoney.com/reviews/personal-loans/credit-ninja)).
- **LendingPoint:** requires a score of 585-600 or higher ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/loans/personal-loans/reviews/lendingpoint)).
- **Cash advance apps:** new users reportedly start around $85-$100 a day at EarnIn, so no single app delivers $1,000 on day one ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/personal-loans/learn/earnin-cash-advance)).
- **Credit union payday-alternative loans:** cheap, but each Texas product found requires 6-12 months of membership or caps at $500 ([Texas Plains FCU](https://www.texasplainsfederal.org/loans/unsecured-loans/); [Neighborhood CU](https://myncu.com/borrow/quick-cash-loan)).

**Advance-fee scams.** No legitimate lender guarantees approval or asks for a fee before funding ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-advance-fee-loans)). Legitimate Texas fees are financed into or deducted from the loan at closing, never paid upfront by wire or gift card.

**Optional add-ons.** Decline optional credit insurance at storefront lenders; it sits outside the rate caps.

## How solid the evidence is

Every figure comes from search-result summaries, because the researchers could not open lender or regulator pages. Much of the detail comes from review and affiliate sites that contradict each other.

- **Best supported:** claims that surfaced from lender-owned pages. These are Oportun's Texas amounts, the OppLoans and Rise Texas APR ranges, Sun Loan's APR range, Possible's $750 cap, and Capital Good Fund's no-minimum-FICO statement.
- **Weakest:** approval odds (no lender publishes rates by score band), funding speed (no verified figure for Oportun, Capital Good Fund or the Community Loan Center), Texas-specific origination fees, and the storefront dollar-cost estimates.
- **Not checked:** the licence status of every lender with the Texas Office of Consumer Credit Commission.
- **Unknown:** whether Fokus participates in the Community Loan Center, the borrower's existing debts, and the age of the bank account.

## Conclusion

The lenders most willing to approve this borrower charge two to five times what the cheapest plausible lenders charge. The sequence therefore matters more than any single choice: a triple-digit loan should come only after Oportun, Capital Good Fund, OneMain and Upstart have all declined. Before accepting any offer, get the Truth in Lending APR and total of payments in writing for the exact amount.

The W-2 versus 1099 answer is the fact most worth settling first, because it decides whether tax-return lenders and the employer-based loan are open at all. Whichever loan is taken, choosing one that reports to the credit bureaus — Oportun, World Finance and Rise do — turns it into a route across the 580 line, where pricing falls sharply.
